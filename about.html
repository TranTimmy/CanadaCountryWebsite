About Canada

Learn and explore what Canada has to offer! The table provided below lists a small part of what Canada's grand package contains!

Foods: Poutine Maple Syrup Montreal Bagels Nanaimo Bars
Animals: Beavers Moose Bison Common Loon
Tourist Attraction: Niagara Falls Banff National Park Capilano Suspension Bridge Park Jasper National Park
National Holidays: Canada Day (July 1st) Victoria Day Thanksgiving National Indigenous Peoples Day (June 21st)
Brief History: Viking settlements around 1000 CE French and British colonization in the 1500s-1700s Confederation in 1867, creating the Dominion of Canada Full independence achieved in 1982 with the Constitution Act
